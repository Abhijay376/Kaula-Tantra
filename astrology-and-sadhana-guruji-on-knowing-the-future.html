Astrology and Sadhana: Guruji on Knowing the Future

In a May 2006 talk at Devipuram, Guruji asks what astrology can tell us about the future, and why sitting in peace may matter more.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Post title: "Astrology and the Environment", a discourse Guruji gave in May 2006 at Devipuram, posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 5 November 2016. The original also has a photo of Guruji, which is not copied here. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • Guruji starts his talk on astrology and sadhana with time. You can know the past but cannot change it. You can change the future but cannot know it.
  • He calls the past His-story, which belongs to the Father, and the future Her-story, which belongs to the Mother. The present is where knowing and acting meet.
  • People turn to astrology because not knowing the future makes them afraid. Sometimes "the stars are bad" is just a handy excuse.
  • Nobody says exactly what "time of birth" means, which is why many people call astrology a pseudo-science.
  • He sees every person and every planet as alive and affecting each other. Sensing that takes sadhana, and he says "For sadhakas, astrology works 100 percent."
  • Once you know you are the Truth, effort drops away and you can simply be at peace.

Guruji's words

A discourse by Guruji Sri Amritananda Natha
May 2006, at Devipuram

You can change the future, but you cannot know it. You can know the past, but you cannot change it. The present is the interface between knowing and changing, between knowing and acting. The past is history, the future a mystery. The past is His-story and the future is Her-story. The future belongs to the Mother and the past belongs to the Father.

It would be nice to know the future. All fear is caused by not knowing what the future holds. So people try to dispel its mystery by various means, such as astrology. And astrology can sometimes be a convenient thing, too! Say somebody has made a marriage proposal to your daughter. You don’t like the match, but you don’t want to take the blame for rejecting it. So you say, “The stars are bad; what can we do?”

As a result, many people conclude that astrology is based on myth. And it seems like a pretty good conclusion, doesn’t it? Think about it: “What is your time of birth?” Well, what does that mean? Is it the time when the sperm meets the egg? Or is it when life occurs in the womb, which is the third month? Or is it when the head comes out of the womb? Or when the whole body comes out? Or when you cut the umbilical cord? What is the precise time of birth? Nobody specifies. What for? That’s why they call astrology a pseudo-science.

But if we understand that every human being has a life, and that every planet has a life, and that each life interacts somehow with all other lives and therefore exerts some influence – well, then we can begin to understand astrology. The solar system has a life. And within that living entity, each planet is influencing the system’s other entities in subtle ways that don’t depend upon distance. To understand these interactions, or to get the right intuition about them, you need to do sadhana. For sadhakas, astrology works 100 percent. For people who want to earn money, it’s all bunkum. There is a science and a non-science to astrology. And that is the difference between them.

So you do your sadhana. And once you realize that you are the Truth, what need have you to do more? Once you realize that all efforts take you away from yourself, then you can remove all the effort and just be what you are. What sadhana am I doing? I’m sitting and talking. I’m not doing japa. I’m not doing anything, just easing myself into a state of peace. That is what I’m doing.

"Astrology and the Environment", a discourse by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati, May 2006 at Devipuram, as posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 5 November 2016.

What this means in practice

Guruji says the future belongs to the Mother. If you worry about what is coming, you can bring that worry to the Devi in your daily puja and leave it there. Your own work is in the present, which he calls the meeting point of knowing and acting.

He treats the solar system as one living being, with each planet touching the others in subtle ways. Kaula practice honours nature and the five elements as alive, and in this talk the planets belong to that same living world.

His last words are about effort. He describes his own practice in that moment: sitting, talking, no japa, just easing into peace. You can end your own sadhana the same way, with a few minutes of sitting with no mantra and nothing to do.

Common questions

Does astrology really work, according to Guruji?

He says "For sadhakas, astrology works 100 percent. For people who want to earn money, it's all bunkum." For him the difference is sadhana, which gives you the right intuition about how lives act on each other.

Why is astrology called a pseudo-science?

Guruji points to the time of birth. Is it when sperm meets egg, when life starts in the womb in the third month, when the head comes out, or when the cord is cut? Nobody specifies, and that is why people call it a pseudo-science.

Can you change your future?

Yes. In his words, "You can change the future, but you cannot know it." The past works the other way: you can know it but cannot change it.

Why do people go to astrologers?

Guruji says all fear comes from not knowing what the future holds, so people try to clear up the mystery. He adds that the stars can also be a convenient excuse, for example to turn down a marriage proposal without taking the blame.

What does "His-story and Her-story" mean?

It is Guruji's play on words. The past is history, His-story, and belongs to the Father. The future is a mystery, Her-story, and belongs to the Mother.
