What Is Sri Chakra? Guruji on Its Nine Layers and Their Gifts

Guruji's short answer to two questions people ask about the Sri Chakra: what it is, and what a practitioner gets from it.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • What is Sri Chakra? Guruji says sages saw it in meditation. It brings together geometry, sound and life to channel the formless powers of God.
  • He calls it the king of yantras. Nine layers of Shaktis cover the light at its centre, and their nickname, Khadgamala, means a garland of swords.
  • Each layer, from the outer square to the central point, comes with its own gift, from dream insight to freedom from disease and union with Shiva and Shakti.
  • Guruji says all of this needs the Guru's grace and your own steady effort.
  • He gives a graded course: Ganapati, Syama, Varahi, then Lalita. Practise each for at least a week and do not skip levels.

Guruji's words

Guruji: For thousands of years, sages in India and other parts of the world meditated to discover sacred (=self-organising) life principles—how to enhance happiness and reduce misery in life. Śrī Cakra arose in their visions. It combined geometry, sounds, and life to channel the nameless, formless powers of God.

It is the king of yantras, with nine layers of Śaktis (powers) covering the light of God in the forms of stars, lotuses, and squares containing powers, passions, and beauties. Their unmasked beauty and razor-sharp intellects earned them a nickname—Khaḍgamālā—a garland of swords. They cut off and pierce through all limiting ideas of “I” and “mine” to release your unbounded bliss of God.

Learn the secret teachings of Sa-maya (=Śakti’s happiness), Dakṣiṇa (=Śiva’s happiness), or Kaula (=both happinesses), and Vāma (=offering self in fire) ācāras, and finally Ānanda Bhairavīs of devotees forming into self-empowering maṇḍalas of Śrī Cakras. The Guru at the center of such a circle is called Mahā Maṇḍaleśvara.

Meditate on names and forms of Avaraṇa (covering) Devīs as you go inwards to get the following.

Layers of coverings in Śrī Cakra:

Square—10 siddhis, 8 passions, 10 gestures. Use them to enjoy astral powers.

Lotus of 16 petals. Time is divided into 16 lunar days, each day bringing a special dream gift.

Lotus of 8 petals. Let go of inhibitions. Offer fruits of all your actions to Goddess.

Star of 14. Powers ruling the worlds place their riches at your feet.

Outer star of 10. These airy spirits blow away poverty.

Inner star of 10. They defend you from enemies.

Star of 8. They eliminate diseases.

Triangle. They teach secrets of controlling lust, enhancing wisdom, and manifesting visions.

Circle or point. Take you to unending powers beyond mind. Merge with infinite peace, bliss, and light of union with Śiva–Śakti.

To get everything stated here, Guruji’s grace is necessary, followed by your untiring effort.

The role of a Guru is to enlighten you. The Śrī Cakra is an oceanic subject, confusingly intricate. So the Guru gives you a graded course consisting of Gaṇapati, Śyāmā, Vārāhī, and Lalitā. You should learn one, and after practicing it for at least a week, move on to the next level. Skipping levels is not recommended.

There are innumerable side attractions on the spiritual journey. Guru is like a compass, guiding you through his mantra about what to enjoy and what to avoid. Seek Guru’s guidance astrally with his mantra.

What this means in practice

The nine layers are the map behind Navavarana puja. In outer worship you move from the square to the point on a yantra in front of you. In inner worship the same journey is made in your own awareness, layer by layer, until only the centre is left.

Guruji's order of learning is practical. Ganapati clears the way, then Syama, Varahi and Lalita follow. A week with each before moving on gives the practice time to settle. Our pages on Ganapathi Pooja, Syama Pooja and Vaarahi Pooja describe these steps.

He also lists the Samaya, Dakshina, Kaula and Vama ways of practice. They are named here only as he names them. Such paths are learned from a living teacher, at the level of meaning first, and any shared practice happens only between willing, consenting adults.

Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015. Guru Amma Annapurnamba teaches today.

Common questions

What is Sri Chakra?

Guruji describes it as a yantra that sages saw in meditation. It combines geometry, sound and life to channel the nameless, formless powers of God. He calls it the king of yantras.

What are the nine layers of Sri Chakra?

From the outside in: the square, the lotus of 16 petals, the lotus of 8 petals, the star of 14, the outer star of 10, the inner star of 10, the star of 8, the triangle, and the central circle or point.

What are the benefits of Sri Chakra worship?

Guruji links each layer to a gift: dream insight, freedom from inhibition, riches, relief from poverty, protection from enemies, freedom from disease, wisdom, and finally union with Shiva and Shakti. He says this needs the Guru's grace and steady effort.

What does Khadgamala mean?

Khadgamala means a garland of swords. Guruji says the Shaktis of the Sri Chakra earned this name because they cut through limiting ideas of "I" and "mine".

In what order should Sri Chakra practices be learned?

Guruji gives a graded course of Ganapati, Syama, Varahi and Lalita. Learn one, practise it for at least a week, then move on. He advises against skipping levels.
