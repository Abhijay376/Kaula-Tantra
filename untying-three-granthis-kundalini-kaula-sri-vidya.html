Kundalini and Sri Vidya

Untying the Three Granthis: How Kaula Tantra and Sri Vidya Dissolve Kundalini Blockages Without Force

Why pushing at kundalini blocks tends to backfire, and what Guruji taught instead.

From the teachings of Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji) and the Devipuram line, as I understand them. I am not a teacher. This is a study note, not instruction.

In short

  • Many people treat kundalini like a wall to break through. Forcing it often tightens the ego and upsets the nervous system.
  • Guruji taught that the body is a temple and that Kundalini is coherent awareness, not an enemy to beat.
  • The three granthis (knots) are false ideas about who we are, not lumps in the spine.
  • Clearing the path and untying a knot are two different jobs. One is a quick sweep. The other takes years.
  • Love, surrender and steady practice loosen the knots. Force does not.
  • If you feel panic, chest pain or fear, see a doctor first. This site gives no medical advice.

Where I started

Ten years ago I was stuck in a body I could not get out of. Pain everywhere. Panic attacks. I could not catch my breath. One day I sat with my eyes and ears closed and put my attention on the breath. I heard my heartbeat clearly. Under it there was a sound like a volcano erupting in my chest, and a wheel turning at the heart centre. I had no name for it.

A friend told me it was kundalini jagarana and pointed me to Devipuram. That is how I ended up studying Sri Vidya. I am still learning. I tell you this because the first thing I want to say is simple. What I felt was not a battle. And the people I meet often talk about kundalini as one.

They talk about breaking through a block along the spine. They hold the breath hard and push with willpower. In my understanding, this tends to backfire. It tightens the ego, because now "I" am the one doing the breaking. It can also unsettle the nervous system. If you are dealing with panic, please see a doctor before any breath practice.

What Guruji said instead

Guruji, Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati, was a nuclear physicist before he became a Kaula Sri Vidya master and founded Devipuram. His way of putting it was plain. The body is a living temple. Kundalini is coherent awareness, not something dangerous you have to fight. The knots loosen through recognition, love and surrender. Not through violence against the body.

What are the three granthis?

A granthi is a knot. Not a lump in the spine. It is a deep habit of the mind, a false idea about who we are, that keeps awareness stuck on appearances. I am giving you the grouping the way I learned it in this line. Other schools place the knots a little differently.

KnotWhereThe false ideaWhat it feels like
Brahma granthiRoot and sacral (Muladhara, Svadhishthana)"I am my body."Fear of death, money and safety worries, bodily shame.
Vishnu granthiSolar plexus and heart (Manipura, Anahata)"I am my relationships and my power."Control, dependence on others, winning and losing, spiritual pride.
Rudra granthiThroat and third eye (Vishuddha, Ajna)"I am a separate observer."Clever arrogance, and the feeling of standing apart from everything.

Clearing the path is not the same as untying the knot

There are two jobs here, and they get mixed up.

Clearing the path is like sweeping the road before you walk it. You do it before a sitting, to calm what is in the way right now: restlessness, heaviness, fear, cravings. It is temporary, and you repeat it.

Untying a granthi is a change in how you see yourself. In this line it comes over time, through steady japa of the Panchadashi and Shodashi, through inner worship, and through fearless surrender. You do not do it in a weekend.

The six helpers before japa

In the Devipuram line, six obstacle-removing mantras are said once before japa. Each belongs to a chakra and a helping deity, as in the study of the Parashurama Kalpasutra that I have read. They clear the impurities one centre at a time, from the root upward. I am leaving the mantras themselves out. They are taught in person, with guidance.

  • Root (Muladhara), with Iravati: wakes the energy and clears the three basic impurities.
  • Sacral (Svadhishthana), with Tripura Bhairavi: protects the three bodies and turns creative energy toward the path.
  • Solar plexus (Manipura), with Samhara Bhairava: burns off dullness and heaviness.
  • Heart (Anahata), with Raktayogini Vallabha: wins over fear instead of fighting it.
  • Throat (Vishuddha), with Sarasa: clears greed for sensations, so you take only the essence.
  • Third eye (Ajna), with Chamunda: dissolves pride, of the body and of the mind.

Please check the names against your own teacher. I am reading them from a study, not from a person I could ask.

Guruji's chakra affirmations

Guruji also left a guided meditation, Healing Chakras Through Affirmations. In it he pairs a calm attention on each centre with a plain prayer. I am only sharing small pieces. The full meditation is better heard in his own voice.

A few lines

Root, the fear of not being safe. He asks the Goddess to lift fear, doubt and guilt, and says: "Goddess is my provider and protector."

Sacral, shame and inhibition. The prayer asks that shame and fear be lifted by love, so that we can love freely and in a healthy way.

Solar plexus, the need to control. "I release the need to control and manipulate my environment."

Heart, peace. "My heart is now filled with love and peace."

His daily lines work the same way. "My body is a temple." Read them on the daily affirmations page.

How to practise, gently

Put your attention lightly on one centre. Breathe in an easy, smooth way. Do not hold the breath hard. Ask the Goddess to lift the tightness, instead of fighting the feeling. That is all.

If something moves

People sometimes feel heat, tingling or small movements as things loosen. Guruji's advice was to drop the fear, watch without resisting, and let the energy settle. I would add this. If anything feels worrying, or does not settle, stop and speak to a doctor. Do not push through it.

For deeper work, find a teacher. Devipuram runs Sri Vidya courses online, and I learned from them. Guruji's own writing is on his blog, linked below.

What I take from all this

Kundalini does not need to be forced open, because Shakti is already your own deepest nature. When fear gives way to devotion and kindness toward yourself, the knots loosen. That is the teaching I try to live, one small step at a time.

Common questions

What are the three granthis?

They are three knots, or deep contractions, in how we see ourselves. Brahma granthi is the belief "I am my body". Vishnu granthi is the belief "I am my relationships and my power". Rudra granthi is the belief "I am a separate observer".

Is a granthi a physical block in the spine?

No. In this teaching a granthi is a habit of the mind, a false idea of who we are. It is not a lump you can find or push through.

Why does forcing kundalini often backfire?

Hard breath holds and sheer willpower can tighten the ego and upset the nervous system. If you feel panic, chest pain or fear, stop and see a doctor first.

Can I do this without a teacher?

The gentle parts, such as kind affirmations and easy breathing, are fine to try on your own. Mantra work and deeper practice are best learned from a teacher in the lineage.

What if I feel heat, tingling or movement during practice?

Guruji's advice was to drop the fear, watch without resisting, and let it settle. If anything feels worrying or does not settle, stop and get medical advice.
