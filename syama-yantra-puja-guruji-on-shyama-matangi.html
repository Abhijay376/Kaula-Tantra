Syama Yantra Puja: Guruji on Shyama, Mother of Music

Guruji's notes on the goddess Shyama, Lalita's minister of the arts, followed by the full order of her yantra puja as he gave it.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • Syama yantra puja is the worship of Shyama, also called Matangi, on her yantra. Guruji calls her the cultural minister of Lalita and the mother of music.
  • He tells how she wrote the seed syllable aim on the poet Kalidasa's tongue, who then composed the Shyamala Dandakam.
  • Her main work, in Guruji's words, is to turn lust into caring love and to move the focus from the genitals to the heart.
  • He says Lalita is best approached through Shyama, which is why she comes second in his graded course.
  • The rest of the post is the puja itself: Guru mantras, breath work, nyasa, the arghya cups, the enclosures of the yantra, 16 offerings and a bali offering.

Guruji's words

Shyama manages cultural affairs of Lalita, the empress of the cosmos. Her upasana brings out abundance and fine arts to seeker. Idiot Kalidas worshiped Her. She wrote the kali bija aim on his tongue with her tongue. He became a great poet and sang the incomparable musical composition called Shyamala Dandakam. She transforms sexual lust to a caring love. She breast feeds nectar to those burning in the fire of Lalita's never ending desire to enjoy life and procreate called kama bhoga. She is adored in the tender, loving and caring heart center of motherhood in women. Lalita is best approached through Shyama.

Shyame Sangeeta Maathaha-Parasiva Nilaye-Mukhya Saachivya Bhaaro
Dvaahe Dakshe- Daya Puritha Nija Hrudhaye- Mamakeem Dainya Vruttim
Srimat Simhasnesyam-Bhava Vana Patitan Dava Dagdhan Namste
Tratum Piyusha Varshaih Kadhaya Parikaram Baddha Vatyam Vivekthe.

Shyama is the mother of music, blue-green in colour like vegetation. She resides in the heart of Siva . Able Cultural minister of Lalita, Her love is filled with compassion. She alone can cool the heat of lust raged by the Queen Lalita. She shifts focus from genitals to heart. She drenches your body with nectar from her breasts cooling your passions. She likes to embrace and play long hours making love with you in secret. Thus meditate on the tantric goddess Shyama, the blue-green Nila Saraswati.

Nature Shyama is Hladini-Raseswari-Radha, the irresistible love power of Krishna. She is the power inherent in the curly movements of healing and creative touch energies. Unpredictable like the waves of smoke emanating from incense, the love power makes your thinking mind stop and make your body move gracefully to her music from within. When your body itself is used to massage another person's body, serpentine movement of feelings of healing touch replace logical thinking. Then trance yoga happens naturally like with two serpents making love.

This is called the Kundalini, the curvy movement starting from the the pit of genitals called kula-kunda, where the fire of lust rages constantly. Exhausted by the movements, but not emptied of seed by a physical orgasm, you will enter into calm awareness. Then you are oberving, but there is nothing to observe. Orgasm separates from ejaculation, but enjoyment is not separated. Since the seed is withheld or offered into fire if expressed, there is no possibility of birth, and so, no possibility of death either. The resulting cycle of births and deaths is cut, nirvana happens. This is the essence of the Shyama tantra, which quenches the fire of lust through transformation. She heals the body and nourishes the psychic nature through fine arts and music.

What this means in practice

Guruji presents Shyama as the heart side of Sri Vidya. Lalita is desire and the enjoyment of life. Shyama cools that heat and turns it toward care, music and the arts. Many people meet her first through singing, dance or simple tenderness, before any ritual.

The yantra puja he lays out is outer worship: a drawn yantra, cups of water and milk, flowers and the 16 offerings. The same layers can also be held in the mind as an inner journey from the square to the bindu. Our Syama Pooja page describes how we offer this worship.

Some of Guruji's lines describe Kaula forms of the practice involving a partner. We explain those only at the level of meaning. Any shared practice happens only between willing, consenting adults, and it is learned from a living teacher, never from a page.

Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015. Guru Amma Annapurnamba teaches today. Read Ganapati Tarpanam for the step that comes before Shyama in his course.

Common questions

Who is Goddess Shyama?

Guruji describes Shyama as a form of Matangi and the cultural minister of Lalita. She is the mother of music, blue green like vegetation, and she is also called Nila Saraswati.

What are the benefits of Shyama worship?

Guruji says her worship brings abundance and skill in the fine arts. He also says she transforms lust into caring love and heals the body through music and the arts.

What is the Shyama mula mantra?

The post gives it in full, beginning "Aim Hrim Srim Aim Klim Souh, Om Namo Bhagavathi Sri Matangiswari". Guruji gave such mantras at initiation, so it is best learned from a teacher.

What does the Shyama yantra look like?

Guruji lists it from the centre out: bindu, triangle, five pointed star, eight petal lotus, sixteen petal lotus, another eight petal lotus, four petal lotus and a square.

Why approach Lalita through Shyama?

Guruji says Shyama alone can cool the heat of desire that Lalita stirs, so she is the gentle way in. In his graded course she comes after Ganapati and before Varahi and Lalita.
