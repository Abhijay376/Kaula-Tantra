Guruji's teachings

The Two Goals of Spiritual Evolution

A short message from Guruji on what the path is for: a better life for you, and the chance to share what you have learned.

From the teachings of Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Original post: The Two Goals of Spiritual Evolution, Guruji's blog, posted by Vira Chandra on 25 April 2016. The message is signed by Guruji and dated 22 September 2010. The post quotes no other book, author or website.

In short

  • Guruji names two goals: a better quality of life, and becoming a teacher who shares.
  • Spiritual growth starts when your focus moves from yourself to others.
  • Your surroundings then begin to support you with love.
  • The practice connects you to cosmic power for a first taste of wider awareness.
  • Living by values such as sharing and love brings a new life with less stress.
  • Like cells in a body, many people can join in love and sharing to become divine.

Guruji's words

Our goals are:

To give you a better quality life.

To make you a teacher to share with others what you have learned.

Shift your focus from yourself to others, sharing what you have. Spiritual evolution starts happening only then. The idea makes sense, because your environment will start supporting you, sharing love with you. Your life becomes that much easier and enjoyable.

We connect you to the cosmic power to give the first experience of expanded awareness in the shortest time possible. The power unfolds your new life if you live with ethical values like sharing and love. Rid of the stresses, you will enjoy an improved quality of life.

As an example of what can happen, look at your own body. Billions of separate cells in you surrender parts of egos to create you, a better life form than the cells. In the same way, billions of us can join in love and sharing to become divine.

Guruji
22 September 2010

Shown in full, word for word, from The Two Goals of Spiritual Evolution, Guruji's blog, 25 April 2016. Signed in the post: Guruji, 22 September 2010.

How this shows up in Kaula practice

The path starts with the self but does not end there. Guruji says growth begins when attention turns outward, to sharing.

In Kaula practice this means the inner work, such as meditation and mantra, goes together with kindness to other people. The Goddess is in them too.

The first experience of wider awareness is a start. Living by values like love and sharing is how it stays. Practice is taken from a teacher.

Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015. Guru Amma Annapurnamba guides the lineage today.

Common questions

What are the two goals of spiritual evolution?

Guruji names them as giving you a better quality of life, and making you a teacher who shares what you have learned with others.

When does spiritual evolution start?

He says it starts when you shift your focus from yourself to others and share what you have.

What is the cell example in the post?

Billions of cells in your body give up parts of their separate egos to make you, a better life form. In the same way, billions of people can join in love and sharing to become divine.

What does connecting to cosmic power mean here?

Guruji says the aim is to give a first experience of expanded awareness in the shortest time possible. The power then unfolds a new life if you live by values like sharing and love.
