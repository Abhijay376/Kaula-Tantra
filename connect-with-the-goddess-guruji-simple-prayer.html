How to Connect With the Goddess: Guruji's Simple Prayer

A short note from Guruji on what the Goddess is to him, with a prayer anyone can try today.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • To connect with the Goddess, Guruji imagines Her standing on top of his head, then pulls Her inside by the feet.
  • He prays that She replace him fully, so Her thoughts, knowledge and power become his.
  • He says Devi is the consciousness in all of us. She is the reason our eyes see and our ears hear.
  • Her blessing comes when we say yes to our own happiness, because She is happy when we are.
  • He also says: you are the Devipuram, don't run after siddhis, and knowledge is experience.

Guruji's words

Sharing what little I know of Goddess…
That She is the art of creating new, better worlds…
That I feel like a baby sleeping contentedly in Her bosom after being fed by Her…
That She is my presence everywhere, all the time…
That She is born in my idea of Her, yet She gave me that idea first…

I think of Goddess like this. She is standing on top of my head facing the same direction as me. I catch Her feet with my hands and pull Her inside of me. Being a body of light, She comes in easily. Pray that She merges with me totally, replacing me with Her. Then Her thoughts are mine, Her knowledge is mine, Her power is mine. It is thus I pray. Give it a try.

Pray to Śakti to reveal Her secrets. If your compassionate mother doesn’t help you, who will?

Devī is the consciousness present in you, me, and in all of us, and everywhere. Because She is inside of us as life, our eyes see, ears hear, and we are able to propagate life. She is happy when we are happy, and unhappy if we are. So to get Her blessings, we have to say yes to our own happiness.

You are the Devīpuram, where Goddess lives.

Don’t run after siddhis. If we run after them, they will flee, but if we don’t seek them, they will seek you.

Knowledge is nothing but experience. Experience is nothing but totally involving yourself.

"Sharing what little I know of Goddess", by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati, as posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. The text is given in full. The original post includes a photo of Guruji, which is not copied here.

What this means in practice

This is inner worship in its simplest form. There is no yantra, no cup and no mantra to learn. You sit, picture Her above your head, and draw Her in. Guruji ends the instruction with "Give it a try", so he meant it for anyone.

The prayer matches the Kaula view of the body as Her temple. If She lives in you as the life that sees and hears, then caring for your body and your joy is part of the worship. Guruji puts it plainly: to get Her blessings, say yes to your own happiness.

His line about siddhis is a useful check. Practice for the contact with Her, and let powers come or not. Our Body Worship page shows how we work with the body as the Goddess's home.

Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015. Guru Amma Annapurnamba teaches today.

Common questions

How do I connect with the Goddess?

Guruji's way is to picture Her standing on top of your head, facing the same direction as you. Catch Her feet, pull Her inside, and pray that She merges with you fully.

Where does the Goddess live?

Guruji says Devi is the consciousness in you, in everyone and everywhere. In his words, you are the Devipuram, where Goddess lives.

How do I get the Goddess's blessings?

Guruji writes that She is happy when we are happy and unhappy when we are not. So to get Her blessings, we have to say yes to our own happiness.

Should I seek siddhis in sadhana?

Guruji says no. If you run after siddhis they flee, and if you don't seek them, they seek you.

What does Guruji mean by knowledge is experience?

He writes that knowledge is nothing but experience, and experience means involving yourself totally. Reading about Her is a start, but knowing Her comes from doing the practice fully.
