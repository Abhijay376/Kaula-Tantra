Varahi Puja: Guruji's Order of Worship for Kola Mukhi

Guruji's full krama for Varahi, the boar faced commander of Lalita's army, from the midnight start to the closing offerings.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • Varahi puja is the worship of Varahi, also called Kola Mukhi, the boar faced goddess who leads Lalita's army.
  • Guruji places her in the ajna chakra and calls her the one who rules time, removes the bad and protects the good.
  • Every mantra in her puja starts with her crown syllables, aim glaum.
  • The puja begins at midnight with the inner anahata sound, then moves through breath work, many nyasas, nine mudras and the layers of her yantra.
  • Guruji says that after this puja you can keep Devi in your heart, with freedom, ajna chakra siddhi and lasting happiness.

What this means in practice

Guruji's krama is outer worship with a drawn yantra, a cup of arghya, flowers and offerings. Most of the work happens in the body, though. Each nyasa places a piece of her mantra on a part of you, from the feet to above the crown, so the worshipper becomes her seat before the yantra is touched.

He gives Varahi the ajna chakra and the rule of time. That is why people turn to her for protection and for clearing what blocks them. He also notes a small change for healing: say "roga prarooganam" in place of "dushta pradushtanaam" when the aim is to remove disease.

Some steps use wine, fish and meat, and one mudra is read in a Kaula way. We explain those only as meaning here. Any shared practice happens only between willing, consenting adults, and the mantras are learned from a living teacher at initiation.

Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015. Guru Amma Annapurnamba teaches today. Our Vaarahi Pooja page describes how we offer this worship, and Sri Maha Varahi Meditation gives a gentler way in.

Common questions

Who is Varahi Devi?

Guruji calls her Kola Mukhi, the boar faced dandanayaki or army commander of Lalita. She sits on a lion, lives in the ajna chakra, rules time, and destroys the bad while saving the good.

What is the Varahi mantra crown?

Guruji calls aim glaum the Varahi makutam, or crown, and says it comes before every mantra in her puja. He explains it as Aim for Saraswati and Glaum for Ganapati.

When should Varahi puja be done?

Guruji's krama starts at midnight. You get up, listen to the inner anahata sound for a few minutes, and bow to your guru on top of your head before beginning.

What are the benefits of Varahi puja?

Guruji writes that after this puja you can keep Devi in your heart and gain freedom, ajna chakra siddhi, apratihata ajna and lasting happiness. He also gives a variation for removing disease.

How much japa is needed for Varahi mantra siddhi?

The post asks for 1 lakh japa, 10,000 homa, 1,000 tarpanams, 100 marjanams and feeding 10 brahmins. These are traditional figures, best taken up with a teacher.
