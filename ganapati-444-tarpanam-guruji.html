Ganapati Tarpanam: Guruji's 444 Water Offerings

Guruji's morning practice of offering water to Ganapati 444 times, with his own answers on where the number comes from and how long to keep it up.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji). Posted on Guruji's blog by Vira Chandra on 6 November 2016. Originally published at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • Ganapati tarpanam means offering spoons of water to Ganapati with mantra. Guruji's version has 444 offerings and he calls it Caturavritti Tarpana.
  • The day starts with honouring the Guru's feet and seeing Maha Ganapati as an orange sun in the heart.
  • Ganapati is invoked into a small pyramid of turmeric powder on a leaf, and the water is offered with tulasi leaves.
  • The count is 12 with the root mantra, then 8 for each of 28 seed syllables and 8 for each of 26 attending deities. That adds up to 444.
  • Guruji ties the number 4 to the Kali Yuga and to the square base of the pyramid: four directions and four elements, with space above.
  • His full course runs 44 days, with 44 pujas, 2000 homa offerings and feeding 20 poor people. He says to start the offerings right away.

Guruji's words

Get up early in the morning and worship the feet of the Guru on top of your head.

Aim Hrīm Śrīm—Aim Klīm Sauḥ
Haṃsaḥ Śivaḥ So’ham—hasakṣaphrem
Hasakṣa malavarayum hasaum
Sahakṣa malavarayīm sahauḥ
Svarūpa nirūpaṇa hetave svagurave
Śrī Annapūrṇāmbā sahita Śrī Amṛtānandanātha
Śrī Guru Śrī Pādukām Pūjayāmi Tarpayāmi namaḥ

Repeat once more. Imagine that from the feet of the Guru the cool flow of the Ganges water is flowing over your body.

Now imagine that in the center of your heart there is a shining ball of sun, orange in color, rising on the horizon. In that visualize the form of Mahā Gaṇapati, orange in color, and imagine that your whole body is filled with light, the surface of the body is just like a balloon and inside it is the orange light of Gaṇapati, and this glow is coming out of your body too.

Now do your morning oblations and offer a spoon of water to Mahā Gaṇapati in the sūrya (in the sun):

Om Tat Puruṣāya Vidmahe Vakratuṇḍāya Dhīmahi
Tanno Dantīḥ Pracodayāt.

Now finish your morning duties and then sit down facing east and make a pyramid (with a square base) of turmeric powder in a betel leaf or any leaf.

Invoke Gaṇapati into this, saying:

Om Tat Puruṣāya Vidmahe Vakratuṇḍāya Dhīmahi
Tanno Dantīḥ Pracodayāt.

Apply some kuṃkuma to the centers of four triangle sides of the pyramid.

Now take some pure water and put some tulasī in it. With the leaves of the tulasī do the following 444 tarpaṇas. This is the Caturāvṛtti Tarpaṇa. For each Tarpayāmi namaḥ offer a spoon of water on the Gaṇapati.

Sit comfortably on the ground. Keep your awareness at the junction of your seat and ground. Keep the Gaṇapati in front of you and the water to your right.

Clarifications on Gaṇapati Tarpaṇa and the Count of 444

There have been some questions about Gaṇapati tarpaṇa.
Here are the answers.
Hope the following will clear your minds.

1. How does the number of tarpaṇas add up to 444?
12 + 8 × 28 + 8 × 26 = 444.

2. Why 444?
We are in Kali Yuga, which runs on number 4.
Gaṇapati is a pyramid with a square base: East, South, West, North; Earth, Water, Fire, Air. Above the base is the sky = space-time. Point is the unknown origin, sahasrāra. It is unknown because there is no space, no time, no vibrations, no nothing. Like a zero, having no dimensions.

3. Total how many days? 44.
Tarpaṇas per day? 444.
Japas per day? 444.
Pūjās per day? 1.

4. Total how many pūjās? 44.
Āhutis in homa? 2000 (444 × 44 / 10).
Feeding poor? 20.

5. On what days should item 4 be done?
Any day or number of day suitable to you. Preferably towards the end.

6. Should we wait for yantra pūjā to start?
No, start with tarpaṇas right away. 44 pūjās can be finished in the same time.
If not possible, extend it to later time.

What this means in practice

Tarpanam is about the simplest outer worship there is. You need water, a leaf, some turmeric and a mantra. Guruji makes it bigger through repetition and attention: the Guru first, then Ganapati as light in the heart, then the offerings one by one with your awareness resting where you sit on the ground.

In the graded course Guruji gives for the Sri Chakra, Ganapati comes first, before Syama, Varahi and Lalita. These 444 offerings are one way to start. Our pages on Ganapathi Pooja and Unified Consciousness and Ganapathi Tarpanam look at the same deity from other sides.

The 44 day plan with puja, homa and feeding the poor is how Guruji framed it for his students. Mantras like these are best taken from a teacher. Guruji attained mahasamadhi in October 2015, and Guru Amma Annapurnamba teaches today.

Common questions

What is Ganapati tarpanam?

It is an offering of water to Ganapati, one spoon for each mantra ending in "tarpayami namah". In Guruji's method the water goes onto a small pyramid of turmeric that stands for Ganapati.

Why are there 444 offerings?

Guruji gives the sum as 12 + 8 × 28 + 8 × 26 = 444. He says the Kali Yuga runs on the number 4, and the pyramid's square base stands for the four directions and four elements.

How many days is the Ganapati tarpanam practice?

Guruji gives 44 days. Each day has 444 offerings, 444 japa and one puja. Over the course there are 2000 homa offerings and 20 poor people are fed.

What are the benefits of Ganapati tarpanam?

The closing verse in the post lists long life, health, wealth, strength, suppleness, fame, creativity, and enjoyments both material and spiritual.

Do I need to finish yantra puja before starting tarpanam?

No. Guruji says to start the offerings right away. The 44 pujas can be done in the same period or carried on later.
