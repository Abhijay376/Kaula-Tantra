Guruji's Daily Affirmations for Self-Love

Ten short lines Guruji wrote for everyday use, on the sacred body, letting go of guilt, and staying calm through the breath.

Written by Sri Amritananda Natha Saraswati (Guruji), posted by Vira Chandra. Originally published as "Daily Affirmations" at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com.

In short

  • Guruji wrote ten short affirmations meant to be repeated daily, not memorized all at once.
  • The body, including sexuality, is sacred and made by the Goddess.
  • Old guilt belongs to a less aware version of you. Forgive it and move on.
  • You are allowed to love yourself and to want health, joy, and abundance.
  • Remembering the Goddess in good times, not only hard times, keeps you close to Her.
  • Slow, steady breathing is a simple way to release anxiety and come back to the present.

In Guruji's own words

"My body is a temple. All parts of my body are made by Goddess and are divine. They are seats of all powers: manifest and latent."

"Whatever sins I committed, I did in a state of ignorance. Then I was in a hypnosis. Now I am awake... Past is past, gone for ever. I forgive myself, as I forgive all others the wrongs they did to me."

"In every outgoing breath, my anxieties and worries are getting out. With every incoming breath, peace is coming in. I am happy here and now."

These are three of Guruji's ten affirmations, quoted directly. Read all ten in full at the source: "Daily Affirmations," posted by Vira Chandra at amritananda-natha-saraswati.blogspot.com, 2016.

How this shows up in Kaula practice

Kaula practice starts from the idea that your own body is a place of worship, not something to be ashamed of. Seeing the body, including sexuality, as sacred is not a side point in this path. It is close to the center of it. These affirmations are a simple, everyday version of that same inner worship.

Letting go of old guilt fits the same idea. You cannot truly honor the body as a temple while also punishing yourself for the past. Guruji's affirmations ask you to drop that weight so you can actually live the teaching, not just agree with it in theory.

The breath practice at the end is the simplest doorway of all. You do not need a ritual, a yantra, or a teacher in the room to use it. Just noticing the breath, worry leaving and peace arriving, is a small daily habit that supports everything else in the path.

Common questions

What are Guruji's daily affirmations?

They are ten short statements Guruji wrote for daily use. They cover seeing the body as sacred, forgiving yourself for the past, loving yourself, remembering the Goddess, feeling grateful, showing compassion, and staying calm through the breath.

How do I use these affirmations?

Guruji said to pick the ones that speak to you and repeat them every day. You do not need to use all ten. Over time you may notice a real shift in how you feel and act.

Why does Guruji say the body is sacred?

In this teaching, Guruji says every part of the body is made by the Goddess and holds real power. He includes sexuality in this sacredness, not apart from it.

Does forgiving myself mean ignoring my mistakes?

No. Guruji's point is that past actions came from a less aware state of mind. Once you are more awake, holding onto shame does not help you or anyone else. Forgiving yourself is about moving forward, not pretending nothing happened.

How is this connected to Kaula practice?

Kaula practice treats the body itself as a place of worship. Self-love, self-forgiveness, and steady breathing are simple, everyday ways to live that same idea, without needing any ritual tools.
